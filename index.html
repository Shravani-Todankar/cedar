About Cedar

A Forbes-ranked global consulting firm with a 40-year track record across industry sectors.

We assist clients from strategy to execution in creating breakthrough strategy, innovating processes, building performance-oriented organizations, and implementing future-ready business technology for the digital world.

Our heritage includes the former US$1 Billion Renaissance Worldwide, led by creators of the Balanced Scorecard — the world's leading strategy execution & performance management framework.

Our Financial Services & FinTech practice is a Centre of Excellence, delivering client deep knowledge and expertise in transforming their business and technology models in the midst of a digital revolution. Cedar's UK group unit, IBS Intelligence, is a leading pure-play FinTech research, news analysis, and advisory firm; it is the definitive source for analysis of global financial technology markets. IBSi's Sales League Table (SLT) is the global industry barometer ranking BankingTech suppliers.

Built on the expertise of Cedar and IBS Intelligence is Cedar Hill Capital, an independently managed $20-25M fund investing in pre-series A FinTech SaaS Indian companies with a global ambition.
